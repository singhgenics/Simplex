    Dim sum, draught & records

    The same pub with no loft and no stair. Same menu, same cabin-style Chinese, dim sum, bao and Korean bar food, same craft beer and cocktails, same look: a cream-and-charcoal checkerboard, brass lanterns, a reclaimed-wood wall with vintage signs, and a bar that glows through backlit onyx. The whole front of house is now one double-height room, 4.5 m to the concrete slab. The left wall, where the stair was, is seating. Left and right are as you walk in from the road.

    Kitchen · sized to the menu

    Each appliance earns its place against the 22 dishes on the food menu. Nothing is there "just in case".

    • Wok range ×2Chilli chicken, Tangra chilli pork, chilli paneer, honey chilli potato (the toss), mixed chowmein, mixed fried rice, burnt garlic veg fried rice, veg Manchurian gravy and the chilli chicken bao filling. Two woks run at once, which covers 31 covers.
    • Dim sum steamerHar gow, chouriço siu mai, pork xiao long bao, crystal veg dumplings, all three bao and the Steamer for two set. Stacked bamboo baskets over one gas steamer.
    • Twin fryerGolden fried prawns, chonak fish fingers, Korean wings, kimchi fries, honey chilli potato, Manchurian balls, the first fry for the chilli dishes, and the crisp noodles for darsaan. Two baskets keep seafood apart from the rest.
    • 2-burner rangeStock for the chimney soup and hot & sour soup, boiling noodles for chowmein, braising the pork belly for bao, and sauces (gochujang glaze, honey chilli, Manchurian gravy). The chimney pot itself is lit at the table with gel fuel, so it needs no burner here.
    • Rice cooker 10 LRice is cooked ahead and cooled for both fried rices and the cabin combo lunch.
    • ColdOne 2-door reach-in for proteins, prepped veg, fillings, kimchi and sauces. One under-counter freezer under the dumpling bench for folded dumplings, bao, fries, wings and the ice cream for darsaan.
    • PrepTwo benches so raw meat and seafood never share a surface with veg and dumpling folding.
    • DishPre-rinse sink, under-counter dishwasher and a 2-bowl pot sink. Glasses are washed at the bar.
    • Taken outThe second under-counter freezer (one is enough for this menu) and the hood-type dishwasher (an under-counter machine handles 31 covers).
    • Required, not optionalHand-wash sink, insect killer, wet-chemical hood suppression, floor drain with grease trap and the fresh-air supply. FSSAI and the fire NOC expect these.

    Fire & life safety · security

    • StandardLaid out to the National Building Code 2016, Part 4, and the usual Goa Fire NOC checklist. Click Safety above to see every device in red.
    • ExitsFront: glass doors that open outward inside the rolling shutter. Rear: the kitchen door opens outward with a panic bar. The farthest seat is about 7 m from the front door, and everyone is on one level.
    • Simpler approvalsWith no loft there is no mezzanine to classify, no second stair to argue for and no structural deck to certify.
    • ExtinguishersSix: ABC 6 kg + CO₂ 4.5 kg on the niche wall by the bar, CO₂ beside the main electrical board, wet-chemical (Class K) + CO₂ in the kitchen, ABC in the garden.
    • DetectionSmoke detectors across the dining room ceiling and a heat detector in the kitchen. A manual call point and hooter at the entrance, wired to the panel beside the electrical board on the washroom's end wall.
    • CO₂The beer gas cylinder is chained in the back-bar cabinet under the infusion jars, with a low vent grille and a CO₂ alarm mounted low, because leaked CO₂ sinks and collects at floor level.
    • LightingBattery emergency lights at the entrance, the left wall and the aisle to the kitchen. Lit EXIT signs over the front entrance and the rear door.
    • Security5 CCTV cameras recording to a recorder on the storage deck over the kitchen. A small safe under the POS nook.

    Check before you commit

    • LicenceBar licenceBeer and cocktails need a Goa excise licence. Price it before you build the bar.
    • Covers23 inside, 8 outside31 in all, against a break-even of about 10 covers a day. Busy nights fill sooner than with the loft, so the garden matters more.
    • KitchenHood and fanThe canopy hood now gets its full depth. Have a kitchen consultant size the exhaust fan for the wok range, fryer and steamer.
    • StructureStorage deckHave the engineer size the deck for about 2.5 kN/m² storage, fixed to the walls or hung from the slab, and confirm the authority treats it as storage, not a floor.
    • UpkeepHigh ceilingLamps, clouds and frames at 3–4.5 m need a tower ladder for cleaning and relamping.
    • MeasureRear yardThe LPG cage, bins, empty-keg cage, AC units, the bar cooler's compressor and the kitchen duct need about 2 m of yard behind the building, plus 1.1 m clear in front of the rear door. Measure it.
    • AccessRear accessThe plan needs a rear door, space for the LPG cage and bins, and a route for the exhaust duct behind the building.
    • NormsOne unisex washroomCheck that the local body accepts a single unisex washroom at 31 covers, and whether it must be accessible.
    • ServicesMist fansThe garden misting needs a water line, a small pump, a filter and an outdoor-rated power point.
    • LicenceMusicRecorded music in public usually needs PPL and IPRS (or Novex) licences.
