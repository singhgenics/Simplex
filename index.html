    Dim sum, draught & records

    The pub redrawn on the shop's own drawing: 4.9 m wide and 8.8 m deep inside, one double-height room with no loft. Same menu, same cabin-style Chinese, dim sum, bao and Korean bar food, same craft beer and cocktails, same look. The kitchen is rebuilt around the 22 dishes. The chimney leaves through the back wall and turns to the right side, because there is an apartment above, and nothing is stored behind the building, because that is parking. The room is mirrored: the washroom and hand-wash sit on the right wall so they drain straight out to the side, and the bar moves to the left wall. Every walkway is laid out to standard clearances, with at least 0.45 m between any two tables. Left and right are as you walk in from the road.

    Kitchen · sized to the menu

    Checked dish by dish against the 22 dishes on the food menu.

    • Wok range ×2Chilli chicken, Tangra chilli pork, chilli paneer, the honey chilli potato and fish finger tosses, mixed chowmein, both fried rices, veg Manchurian, hot & sour soup and the chilli chicken bao filling. One burner for proteins and sauces, one for noodles and rice.
    • Steamer, 2 holesHar gow, chouriço siu mai, pork xiao long bao, crystal veg dumplings, all three bao and the Steamer for two, in stacked bamboo baskets.
    • Twin-tank fryerPrawns, chonak fingers, Korean wings, kimchi fries, honey chilli potato, Manchurian balls, the first fry for the chilli dishes and darsaan noodles. Two tanks keep veg and non-veg oil apart.
    • 2-burner stock rangeThe six-hour chimney soup stock, the three-hour pork belly braise, the jellied stock for xiao long bao and boiling noodles. The chimney pot itself is lit at the table.
    • Rice cookerDay-old rice for both fried rices and the combo lunch.
    • ColdA slim 2-door reach-in and one under-counter freezer (dumplings, bao, fries, wings, ice cream for darsaan).
    • Taken outThe 0.8 m steamer (a 2-hole unit is enough), the separate pre-rinse table (the spray is on the sink), the pot-wash shelving, and the wider 1.2 m reach-in.

    Outside · right side and back

    • ChimneyAn apartment sits above, so there is no roof stack. The duct leaves the back wall at 2.35–2.75 m, runs right along the back wall, turns the corner and runs forward along the right side wall. It passes an electrostatic precipitator with a carbon odour filter and the fan, then discharges sideways at about 2.6 m, away from the building.
    • GasThe LPG bank (4 × 19 kg) sits in a locked, ventilated cage on the right side, more than 2 m from the exhaust outlet. A copper line runs along the wall and enters the kitchen behind the wok range.
    • BehindParking. Nothing is stored there. The rear door stays as the kitchen's fire exit.
    • Right sideOnly a 0.5 m strip along the wall can be used: cars pass beyond it. Everything is slim: a wall-mounted backup cabinet, a slim keg cage, a slim bin store and a single-row gas cage, with steel bollards along the strip's edge. AC outdoor units and the keg cooler's compressor sit high on the wall. Changing gas and emptying bins means a minute on the pathway, so do it when it's quiet.
    • MeterThe electricity meter stays where it is, on the inside of the right wall just past the front pier. A hinged, brass-framed portrait covers it and lifts open for readings, with a small vent gap behind. The main switch and RCCB move into the distribution board, so nothing you would need in an emergency is hidden behind the art. It sits above the wall-side chairs of the front right table: the chair backs are 0.92 m, well below the meter; move a chair to reach it.
    • DrainsThe WC and the hand-wash basin are on the right wall, so their pipes go straight out below ground into a sealed inspection chamber in the strip, 2 m past the gas cage. The kitchen and bar grey water runs through a grease trap at the back corner. From there one pipe has to reach a treatment tank: see Check before you commit.
    • Power backupA 7.5 kVA online inverter with about 10 kWh of lithium battery, in a slim, ventilated, lockable wall-mounted cabinet (0.35 m deep) at the front end of the right side. It runs the lights, POS and Wi-Fi, the fridges, freezer and coolers, the ice machine, music, CCTV and, most importantly, the kitchen exhaust, ESP and fresh-air fan, so cooking on gas stays safe in a power cut. Roughly 3–4 hours at a normal evening load. The ACs are not on backup. No diesel generator: with the apartment above and parking behind, its noise and exhaust would be a problem.

    Check before you commit

    • MeasurePillars and beamsThe structure positions come from the site video and the old 10.5 m assumption. Mark them on the 4.9 × 8.8 m drawing.
    • ApprovalLow exhaust dischargeA side discharge at about 2.6 m next to parking needs the ESP and odour filter, and usually the panchayat's and the society's agreement. Get it in writing before fitting the kitchen.
    • ConfirmRight side stripEverything outside on the right fits a 0.5 m strip along the wall, with the car pathway beyond. Confirm with the landlord and society that you can use that strip and fix bollards on its edge, and check the fire officer accepts the gas cage beside a driveway.
    • ApprovalSewage treatmentThe 0.5 m strip is too narrow for any tank: a biodigester for a pub this size is about 3,000–5,000 L, roughly 2.5 × 1.2 m. First ask whether Tulip Residency already has a septic tank or sewer line the shop can join (society NOC). If not, a sealed biodigester can go under the car pathway or parking with a traffic-rated cover, but only with the owner's and society's written consent (title deed cl. 9). A soak pit needs 18 m from any well, 1.5 m from foundations and a water table at least 1.5 m below it, which coastal Betalbatim often fails in the monsoon. Expect the panchayat and Goa SPCB to want a sealed system and a consent to operate.
    • LicenceBar licenceConfirm the Goa Excise fee and conditions before building the bar.
    • NormsOne unisex washroomCheck whether 26 covers with alcohol needs separate men's and women's washrooms or an accessible one. There is now one unisex WC and no urinal.
    • StructureStorage deckSize it for about 2.5 kN/m² storage and confirm it is treated as storage, not a floor.
    • ElectricalMeter behind a portraitFine if the cover is hinged or lift-off (not fixed), the meter is never sealed in, and the main isolator sits in the distribution board. Ask the electricity department whether the meter is read remotely (it looks like a prepaid smart meter) and tell them where it is.
    • ElectricalBackup sizingHave the electrician list the backed-up circuits and size the inverter and battery. Wire the gas solenoid so gas cuts off if the exhaust stops.
    • LicenceMusicRecorded music in public usually needs PPL and IPRS (or Novex) licences.
