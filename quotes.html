1Every quote must state

Ask for these in writing. Quotes that leave them out cannot be compared.

  • Steel grade and thickness. 304 stainless only. Suggested: worktops 1.2 mm or thicker, bodies 1.0 mm, legs 38 mm tube. The supplier confirms or corrects this.
  • Make and model of burners, fryer, steamer, compressors, fans and heaters.
  • Litres for each cold unit: the reach-in fridge side and freezer side separately, the under-counter freezer, and the pass fridge. The freezer target is about 650 L in total.
  • Price ex-GST, itemised line by line, with the GST rate shown separately.
  • Delivery to Betalbatim, with freight and unloading, and whether it is included.
  • Installation and gas or water connections, included or extra.
  • Lead time from the advance, and the payment schedule (suggest a part on delivery and inspection).
  • Warranty and who services it in Goa.
  • Validity of the quote, and photos or the address of recent similar work.
On arrival, check before paying the balance
  • Test the steel: 304 is barely magnetic, and a magnet that grips firmly means cheaper 201 or mild steel.
  • Measure each item against this sheet. The aisle is 1.05 m (0.95 m at the wok range), so a few centimetres matter.
  • Outdoor cabinets stand in coastal air. The supplier says whether they are 304 or powder-coated mild steel.
