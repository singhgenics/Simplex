Betalbatim No-Loft Plan

Concept 24 · ground floor only · 4.5 × 10.5 m shell

No loft, no stair: 31 covers

23 seats inside, 8 in a 4.9 × 2.0 m beer garden as wide as the building. The left wall, where the stair was, now holds a window booth and a bench facing the bar. The kitchen is laid out for the 22-dish menu and nothing more. Road at the bottom; left and right as you walk in.
