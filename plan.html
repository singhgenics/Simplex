Betalbatim No-Loft Plan

Concept 25 · ground floor · 4.9 × 8.8 m shell (47 m² super built-up)

The pub on the shop drawing: 28 covers

20 seats inside, 8 in a 5.3 × 2.0 m beer garden as wide as the building. One double-height room, no loft. The kitchen is rebuilt around the 22-dish menu. The chimney leaves through the back wall and turns to the right side, and gas, bins, AC units and empty kegs all sit on the right, because there is an apartment above and parking behind. Road at the bottom; left and right as you walk in.
