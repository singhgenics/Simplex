Betalbatim No-Loft Plan

Concept 27 · ground floor · 4.9 × 8.8 m shell (47 m² super built-up)

The pub on the shop drawing: 26 covers

18 seats inside, 8 in a 5.3 × 2.0 m beer garden as wide as the building. One double-height room, no loft. Mirrored from Concept 25: the washroom and hand-wash now sit on the right wall so they drain straight out to the side, and the bar is on the left wall. The kitchen is rebuilt around the 22-dish menu. The chimney leaves through the back wall and turns to the right side, and gas, bins, AC units and empty kegs all sit on the right, because there is an apartment above and parking behind. Road at the bottom; left and right as you walk in.

OutsideExhaust out the back wall, right along it, round the corner and out sideways at about 2.6 m through an ESP and odour filter. LPG, bins, empty kegs, AC units, the power backup (inverter + lithium battery) and the drains (sealed inspection chamber, grease trap, soil vent pipe) in a 0.5 m strip on the right, behind bollards, with the car pathway beyond. Nothing behind: that is parking.
