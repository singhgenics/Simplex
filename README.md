# Simplex

Cabin East (Wok | Steam | Draught), a Pan-Asian pub in Betalbatim (South Goa), on the shop drawing: a 4.9 × 8.8 m shell (47 m² super built-up), one double-height room with no loft, 26 covers (18 inside, 8 in a 5.3 × 2.0 m beer garden as wide as the building). Concept 27: the washroom and hand-wash sit on the right wall so they drain straight out to the side, the bar is on the left wall, tables for 4 sit end-on to the right wall, and a sofa with three tables for 2 runs along the left wall. The kitchen is sized to the Pan-Asian menu, for one chef and one helper; the kitchen exhaust leaves through the back wall and turns to the right side, where the gas, bins and AC units also sit, because there is an apartment above and parking behind. The bar has 3 taps; the 3 kegs stand in a cabinet inside the bar counter with a flash chiller, so no keg fridge and no keg storage outside.

Live: https://singhgenics.github.io/Simplex/ (opens straight into the 3D model)
