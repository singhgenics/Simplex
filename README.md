# Simplex

The Betalbatim pub (South Goa) on the shop drawing: a 4.9 × 8.8 m shell (47 m² super built-up), one double-height room with no loft, 28 covers (20 inside, 8 in a 5.3 × 2.0 m beer garden as wide as the building). The kitchen is sized to the 22-dish menu; the kitchen exhaust leaves through the back wall and turns to the right side, where the gas, bins, AC units and empty kegs also sit, because there is an apartment above and parking behind. Kegs live in a cooler inside the bar counter.

Live: https://singhgenics.github.io/Simplex/ (opens straight into the 3D model)
