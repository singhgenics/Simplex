How the washroom drains to a biodigester under the garden

Your washroom sits on the right wall, so its pipe goes straight out to the side, runs forward along the 0.5 m side strip under the bins and gas cage, and drops into a sealed tank buried under the right half of the beer garden. Kitchen, bar and hand-wash water take a separate pipe that skips the tank.

Does it need a soak pit?

Yes, something has to take the water that comes out. The biodigester breaks the solids down so the tank rarely needs emptying, but what leaves it is still wastewater, not clean water. There are four ways to deal with it, in order of preference for this site:

  • Join the building's own system. If Tulip Residency has a septic tank, soak pit or sewer connection, send the tank's outflow there with a society NOC. No soak pit of your own.
  • Soak pit, only if the site passes all three tests: at least 18 m from any well, at least 1.5 m from any foundation, and the monsoon water table at least 1.5 m below the pit's bottom (about 3.5 m down). In the drawing it sits beyond the garden, which only works if that land is yours.
  • Planted filter. A lined bed of gravel and reeds or canna can polish the water, for example in place of the front brick planters. It still needs an overflow.
  • Sealed holding tank, pumped out by a tanker when full. Always allowed, but a running cost.

Tanks are sold by "users": a 2,000 L tank is rated for 20 people living in a house. A pub's visitors use the toilet far less than residents, which is why a 2,000 L tank covers 80 guests. Only the toilet goes into the tank; the kitchen, bar and hand-wash water go round it.

Before you dig

  • Confirm the front strip under the garden belongs to the shop, or get the society's written consent (title deed clause 9), and check the CRZ position, as for the pergola.
  • Find out the monsoon water table (a trial pit, or ask the neighbours) and whether any well lies within 18 m.
  • Expect the panchayat and the Goa State Pollution Control Board to ask how sewage is treated; a sealed biodigester is the kind of answer they want.
  • In use: no phenyl, acid or bleach down the WC, no sanitary products or wipes. Mild cleaners only, or the bacteria die.
