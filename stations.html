Kitchen briefing for the chef, helper and vendors

What cooks where

Every dish on the food menu, matched to the station that cooks it. The stations run in line order from the right wall: wok range, steamer, fryer, stock range, then prep, cold and the pass. 25 dishes, 40 choices, one chef and one helper.

Plain: stated in the menu notes likely my pick where the notes do not say. Chef to confirm. decide a choice the menu has not made yet.
Two things to settle before service
  • Wontons: the menu says poached, but the kitchen notes put them on the steamer. Fire Starters and Wonton Kenobi both use them. Poaching needs a stock-range burner at service, and steaming needs a steamer hole.
  • Stock range size: its two burners are asked to run soups, curry bases and noodle boiling at service, while the braise is a morning job. The vendor says 0.6 m is too small for two 20 L pots, so the burner and pot sizes are open.
Source: the food menu and its owner's notes, plus the kitchen drawings. Station sizes are from kitchen sheet 1. The steamer figure of 12 to 13 baskets an hour is a calculation (two holes at about 9 minutes each), not a measured one.
