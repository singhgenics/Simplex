Setup budget: phase 1 before opening, phase 2 after

The whole project, including a 3-month backup, has to stay within ₹12–13 lakh. Phase 1 builds what the kitchen and bar cannot open without, and leaves room, wiring and drains ready for phase 2. In the 3D model the “Phase 2 items” button shows what is added later. These are planning ranges in rupees lakh, not quotes: floor = second-hand or hard bargaining on every line, likely = ordinary new quotes.

Phase 1, floor – likely10.2 – 16.4 L
Backup, 3 months × ₹1.11 L3.3 L
Total at floor prices13.5 L
Ceiling₹13 L

At floor prices on every line the total is ₹13.50 L, which is ₹0.50 L over the ₹13 L ceiling. At likely prices it is ₹19.75 L.

So the plan only fits if nearly every line lands at its floor, and even then there is no contingency. Ways to close the gap, in order of how little they hurt: a licence agent and the brewery's loan terms confirmed in writing before anything is bought; second-hand tables and a local carpenter for the counter; and a backup of 2½ months instead of 3 (₹0.56 L), which is a risk to weigh against the slow first months. Real quotes will replace these figures.

What is not in these numbers

  • The rent deposit and any advance rent, and the Goa excise licence fee, which is still unconfirmed.
  • Any change to the shop shell or a structural fix the society or architect asks for.
  • The hood and gas work depend on the fabricator's burner ratings; a vaporiser or a bigger fan would add to it.
  • Furniture, lighting and signage are my assumption (₹0.9–1.6 L), because the earlier checklist did not price them.
