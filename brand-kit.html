The logo

One pint glass holding the three kitchens: wok and flame, steamer and dumpling, foam on top.

CABIN in cabin red over EAST in ink, set so the two lines are the same width. The tagline is always mono capitals with real pipes and a space either side.

Clear space on every side equals x, the height of the letters in EAST. Minimum size (working guidelines for a designer to confirm): horizontal lockup 40 mm in print or 160 px on screen; icon mark 10 mm or 24 px; roundel 22 mm or 64 px. Below that, use the no-tagline lockup.

The tagline and the voice

WOK | STEAM | DRAUGHT

Always capitals, mono, spaced out, a space either side of each pipe. The pipes may be amber on ink; on cream they stay the colour of the words.

Three words, three kitchens: the wok (Indo-Chinese, Thai, Seoul), the steamer (dim sum, bao), the tap (draught).

Say it like a signpost.

Short, direct, one fact per line, pointing the guest somewhere. Warm, never snobby, never loud.

Open 12 noon – 11 pm. Closed Monday.
Happy hour 12–6 pm.
Wok. Steam. Draught.

Rules

Do

  • Keep the glass, flame and wok, steamer and foam together as one mark.
  • Keep CABIN and EAST the same width, CABIN above EAST.
  • Cream paper by day, ink for night and screens, red for the wok.
  • Keep the tagline in mono caps with spaced pipes.
  • Point every plank east.
  • Leave the clear space (x) around the logo.

Don't

  • No gradients, glows, shadows or outlines on the logo.
  • Don't recolour the pint in other colours: red, ink, amber and cream only, or one colour.
  • Don't stretch, tilt or redraw the mark.
  • No amber or red text on cream below display size.
  • No rounded or script lettering beside the wordmark.
  • No extra pipes or words in the tagline.

Before you print or build

  • Name. "Cabin East" is not trademark-checked. Have an IP advocate search Class 43 (and 30, 32, 33 if you may sell sauces, beer or spirits) before spending on signage.
  • Logo. The vector files are a strong working version built from open fonts. Have a designer finalise the marks.
  • Signage. Sizes are suggestions. Measure the frontage and confirm what permissions signage needs (the panchayat, and the consent clause in the deed): not verified here.
  • Print colour. Ask the printer for a proof against the swatches; do not trust screen colour.
  • Music and licences. Recorded music needs PPL and IPRS (or Novex) licences. Not covered by this kit.
