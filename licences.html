Three rules to follow all the way through

  1. No building until every permission in Phase 3 is in your hands. That means the builder's signed consent (everything outside the shop walls is his), the owner's written consent, the panchayat permission, and any planning, pollution, health and coastal approvals that apply.
  2. One name everywhere. The proprietor's name and the trade name must be spelled exactly the same on the rent agreement, PAN, bank, GST, trade licence, FSSAI and excise licence.
  3. The rent-free period starts only when all the written permissions are in your hands (the builder's consent and everything in Phase 3). No rent, lock-in or term runs before that date.
