Three open items that can stop the excise licence
  • The builder will not allow a septic tank. A biodigester and soak pit also sit outside Shop 4's walls, so they need his written consent and a marked location. Without a lawful sewage arrangement the Health NOC and Pollution consent are at risk.
  • Shop 4 has no toilet inside. Find out who owns the WCs in the stilt area and whether customers can use them.
  • The deed (clauses 9 and 11) needs the builder's written consent for works, exhaust, gas bank and signage.

Order that matters: owner documents and builder's consent, rent agreement, interior permission, Health, Fire and Pollution NOCs, trade licence, FSSAI, excise licence. Plan 4 to 6 months. Rent should start only after the trade and excise licences are issued.

Depends on the office: the sequence of trade licence and FSSAI, whether electricity and LPG connections need the Fire NOC or trade licence first, and when the Pollution "Consent to Establish" is wanted. Ask the panchayat secretary for the exact trade licence document list on the first visit.

Fees are ballpark estimates from secondary sources. Confirm each at the issuing office. Not legal advice; have a local advocate review the title and the rent agreement. Licences go in the name of the tenant entity, not the owner.
