Exact model, textured model and photoreal view

Each view has three states from the same camera. Exact model: built from the sheet's sizes. Textured model: the same model with real photographs (tile, stainless, doors, fronts) wrapped on it, so sizes stay exact. Photoreal: a Google Flow image made from the model image, with the sizes in the prompt. Tap an image to cycle. Generated images are close but not exact, so the sheet and the model govern the sizes.

Sizes are the sheet's W × D × H in mm (equipment list, sheet 1) and its stated heights and positions. Hand-wash, bins, extinguishers and small kit are drawn at their stated sizes; items with no size on the sheet (rice cookers Ø 260 mm, steamer baskets Ø 260 mm, torch, mixer) are drawn at typical sizes. Ducting, ESP, fan, hoist and water heater outside the room are not drawn. Positions from the 3D model: check walls and pillar P2 on site.
