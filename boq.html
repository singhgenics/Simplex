Bill of quantities (BOQ)

90 priced lines in 8 trades, numbered A.01 to H.06, taken from the model and the two A3 sheets. Each line gives the item or appliance type, its size (W × D × H in mm), and its capacity or rating. Suppliers fill the rate, make and model; the Excel file multiplies and totals. Quantities marked “measure on site” are estimates for pricing. Concept drawings for briefing suppliers, not construction drawings.
