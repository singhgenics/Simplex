Every quote must state
  • Steel grade and SWG for each part; make and model, kW or litres for every branded item; the static pressure the exhaust fan is selected for.
  • Price ex-GST with the GST rate separate; delivery to Betalbatim and unloading; installation and connections included or extra; whether the hood and benches are priced per unit or per running foot, and the duct per square foot.
  • Lead time, payment schedule, warranty and who services it in Goa, and how long the quote is valid.
